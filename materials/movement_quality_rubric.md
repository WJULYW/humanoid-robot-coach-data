# Movement Quality Rating Rubric

Two licensed fitness coaches, who were not told the assigned conditions, rated each episode from synchronized dual-view recordings with audio. They first scored all episodes independently and then discussed disagreements to reach one consensus score per episode, which is the `movement_quality` variable in `data/episode_data.csv`.

The rubric covers movement structure, body and joint position, effective range of motion, trajectory and control, alignment of resistance with the intended movement, and visible compensation. The table below is an English translation of the rubric used in the study.

| Score | Level | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Poor | The movement clearly deviates from the standard movement structure. Key joint positions or the movement path show clear errors, and the effective range of motion is severely limited. The direction of resistance is clearly misaligned with the intended line of force of the movement, and movement control is poor. |
| 2 | Below Standard | The intended movement is recognizable, but its structure shows clear deviations. The effective range of motion is insufficient. The movement path or line of resistance shows persistent deviations, and movement stability and continuity are poor. |
| 3 | Acceptable | The basic movement structure is performed correctly, with an adequate effective range of motion. The overall movement path and line of resistance are generally stable, although minor deviations in joint position, movement path, or control remain. |
| 4 | Good | The standard movement structure is maintained well, with a sufficient and effective range of motion. The movement path is stable, and the line of resistance is generally aligned with the intended movement. Only minor deviations that do not affect overall movement quality are present. |
| 5 | Excellent | The standard movement structure and good body and joint positions are maintained throughout. The range of motion is sufficient. The movement path is continuous, stable, and controlled. The line of resistance is closely aligned with the intended movement, with no clear compensation or technical deviations. |
